# GreenRah: 3-minute pitch script

Speaker: Yousef (Joseph A), founder. Audience: investors, the public, urban mobility and city partners.

**Spoken words: 449** (about 3:00 at 150 words a minute). The deck auto-advance (press A) always takes 180 s and splits that time by these word counts.

## 1. Hook  (44 words, about 18 s)

Heat killed an estimated 62,775 people in Europe in summer 2024. Maps cannot see it. Twenty-two percent of people in the EU are 65 or older, those most at risk. Hot days above 30 degrees are set to double, or almost quadruple, this century.

Sources:
- 62,775 heat-related deaths, Europe, 1 Jun to 30 Sep 2024, 32 countries (95% CI 36,765 to 84,379). Janoš et al., Nature Medicine, 2025: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-025-03954-7
- ISGlobal press release: 62,700 heat-related deaths in summer 2024: https://www.isglobal.org/en/-/62.700-muertes-asociadas-con-el-calor-del-verano-de-2024
- Backup if asked about 2023: 47,690 deaths in 2023, 35 countries. Gallo et al., Nature Medicine, 2024. (The 2025 paper re-estimates 2023 at 50,798 with a new dataset.): https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-024-03186-1
- 22.0% of the EU population aged 65+ on 1 January 2025. Eurostat, Population structure and ageing: https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/statistics-explained/index.php?title=Population_structure_and_ageing
- Hot days above 30 °C expected to double (RCP4.5) and almost quadruple (RCP8.5) during the 21st century. EEA, Europe’s changing climate hazards: extreme heat: https://www.eea.europa.eu/en/analysis/publications/europes-changing-climate-hazards-an-index-based-interactive-eea-report/heat-and-cold/heat-and-cold-extreme-heat

## 2. The problem  (34 words, about 14 s)

Imagine you travel to Barcelona, the Algarve or Rome. Google and Apple Maps show you the fastest way. Not the weather. Not the sun hours. Not the shade. So you walk in full sun.

## 3. The solution  (41 words, about 16 s)

So we built GreenRah. Not the fastest way: the coolest way. Our comfort engine combines five layers: live weather, the sun's position, 3D buildings, elevation and slope, and the city's walking network. Out comes a comfort score and a shade list.

Sources:
- Product description supplied by the founder (GreenRah comfort engine, five layers).: https://greenrah.com/app

## 6. Features  (48 words, about 19 s)

On your route and across the map, GreenRah shows water taps and toilets, in Europe and Great Britain. In a heatwave, that is safety, not a detail. It factors in clouds, speaks English and Portuguese, and covers 28 countries and over 2,000 cities. We launched two months ago.

- Coverage (27 EU countries + Great Britain, 2,000+ cities), languages EN and PT, launch about two months ago: founder-supplied product facts.: https://greenrah.com/app

## 7. For everyone  (14 words, about 6 s)

It is for everyone who walks: commuters, tourists, seniors, parents, disabled people, and runners.

## 8. Competition  (58 words, about 23 s)

Some apps show shade. Some show weather. Only GreenRah uses both to choose your route. ALKOO by NAVITIME has shade routes, a shade rate, water spots and cooling shelters. Forecasts and elevation are on Premium. Komoot Premium has route weather, surface type and an elevation profile, but no shade routing. Both inform the user. GreenRah changes the route.

Sources:

## 9. City Insights  (99 words, about 40 s)

Six questions for a mayor. Where is it too hot? Where do people walk? Do they choose the cool route? Service gaps: water, green space, GIRA bikes beyond 400 metres. Who is left out, by freguesia? Did the project help? That is next. Walking trips, not surveys. A 24-hour delay. No personal data. Consultant heat studies cost 15 to 80 thousand euros and are old on arrival. The City pack is 6,000 euros a year and updates daily. Example: people avoid the street from one to five, so a shop open until seven only gets the last two hours.
